A hostile `.gitmodules` pointing to a readable private local repository can now cause `git submodule update --init` to copy its objects into the workspace. Filesystem restrictions still apply. **Fix:** deny non-file transports while preserving Git’s user-only file policy, and test explicit clone success alongside automatic submodule rejection.
