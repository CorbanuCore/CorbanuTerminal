+## Custom providers on OpenAI's regional API hosts
+
+### `/model` after `/providers`
+
+- **A provider restored in `/providers` appears in an already-open model
+  picker** (#2102). Provider discovery finishes in the background; when it
+  settled after the Models menu was open, the menu kept its earlier rows, so
+  OpenAI could stay missing until it was reopened. The open picker now
+  refreshes in place on the tab you were on. A late result does not reopen a
+  dismissed menu or replace an open reasoning-effort dialog.
